Now let me explain how this process actually works, because a lot of fans think a flag automatically equals a fine, and it doesn't. Every roughing the passer call gets reviewed by the league office after the game. They look at the timing, the force, where the contact landed, and whether the defender had a realistic chance to stop. If they decide it deserves more than the on-field penalty, the player gets fined. Under the NFL's fines schedule, a first-time roughing the passer offense comes with a fine of $17,911. Those decisions are usually announced after the week of games wraps up, so we should hear something soon. And if Collins does get fined, he has the right to appeal, which players do all the time, sometimes successfully.
